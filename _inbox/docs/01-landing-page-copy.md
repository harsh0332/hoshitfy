## 11. FAQ (objections)

**"Won't AI editing look cheap and robotic?"** No. Around 80% of every
video is edited by human editors. AI only helps with B-roll and visual
consistency. Your audience sees a polished video, not "AI content."

**"My nephew or an intern can use CapCut."** Most people can use CapCut.
You're not paying for the software. You're paying for reliable delivery
every week, a professional look, and the authority that consistency
builds.

**"You're not in Dubai. How do we work together?"** Our production team
is in India and works on Dubai hours. You share footage on Google Drive,
get updates on WhatsApp, and meet us weekly on Google Meet. Being remote
is exactly why we deliver in 21 hours.

**"I already have an editor."** Keep them. Many clients use us for
overflow, so content never stops when their editor is overloaded or
away.

**"What if I don't like the edits?"** You see your first video free
before paying anything. After that, each video gets 2 revision rounds.

**"It looks expensive."** Compare it to 8–15 hours of your time every
week, or to the salary, visa and management of a full-time editor. On
the call, we'll show you the numbers for your business.

**"Will the price go up after a month or two?"** No. Your plan price
stays locked for as long as you stay with us.

**"Is my footage safe?"** Yes. We sign an NDA, store files encrypted,
and you own everything.

**"I need to think about it."** That's fine. Start with the free edit.
There's nothing to decide until you've seen the result.
